A single_cover is one complete, unified front-cover or poster composition. Multiple characters or integrated artistic montage can still form one cover. A tall canvas alone is not sufficient. Reject two or more independently framed landscape images stacked vertically, repeated cover/title panels, tiled promotional images, screenshot collections, and contact sheets. Use stacked for independent images placed above/below each other, collage for assembled independent panels, screenshot for gameplay/UI captures, and packaging for unfolded front/back/spine scans or photos of product packaging. Use other for unrelated artwork and unknown when uncertain. Do not crop, stitch, or imagine missing artwork to turn a rejected image into a cover.
